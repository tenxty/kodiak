How times are figured (terrain-based): each leg's pace starts from Ty's flat pace and rises with that leg's climbing per mile, up to the steep pace on the Sugarloaf climb. Aid-station stops are added at each station. Whatever time is left in the goal becomes a gradual slowdown over the race. Other goals scale the whole shape up or down.

Climbing per leg comes from the sheet, except the first leg (Rim Nordic to Crab Flats: +1,135 / −1,820 ft), which comes from the race's official aid station chart. Descents count as flat.

Times round to the minute and assume Pacific time. Cushion compares Ty's arrival with each cutoff.

Cushion colors: green is 2+ hours ahead of the cutoff, amber is 45 minutes to 2 hours, red is under 45 minutes.
